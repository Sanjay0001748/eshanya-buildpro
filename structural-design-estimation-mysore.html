Structural Design, Estimation & Costing in Mysore (Mysuru)

Foundation, column, beam and slab design, steel and concrete quantities, and an itemised cost estimate for your building in Mysuru (Mysore).

The structure is the part of a building you cannot fix later. Our structural engineers design the foundation, columns, beams and slabs for your building, and prepare the steel schedule the site works from.

From the same design we work out the steel and concrete quantities and an itemised cost estimate. You see where the money goes before construction starts, and the quantities on site match the quantities in your budget.

What our structural and estimation service covers

  • Foundation design for your building
  • Column, beam and slab design
  • Steel schedule for use on site
  • Steel and concrete quantities
  • Itemised construction cost estimate
  • Structure coordinated with the architectural plan

How it works

  1. Share your plan. Send us the architectural plan, or let us prepare one for you.
  2. Structural design. We design the foundation, columns, beams and slabs.
  3. Quantities. We work out the steel and concrete the structure needs.
  4. Itemised estimate. You receive a cost estimate broken down item by item.

Who it is for

Owners who want an engineer-designed structure, and anyone who wants to know the real cost of a building before committing to it.
