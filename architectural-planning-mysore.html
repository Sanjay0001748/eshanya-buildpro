Architects & Building Planning in Mysore (Mysuru)

Floor plans, site layout, drawings prepared for approval and working drawings for your house or building in Mysuru (Mysore).

A good building starts with a plan that fits the plot, the people who will live or work in it, and the rules it has to meet. Our architects prepare the plan for your site in Mysuru and take it through to the drawings the builders actually use.

Because the same office also does the structural design and the estimate, your plan is drawn with the structure and the budget in mind. That avoids the common problem of a plan that looks right on paper but has to be changed once the engineer or the estimate arrives.

What our planning service covers

  • Floor plans for each level of the building
  • Site layout showing how the building sits on your plot
  • Drawings prepared for building plan approval
  • Working drawings for use on site
  • Coordination with the structural design from the same office
  • Revisions discussed with you before drawings are finalised

How it works

  1. Share your plot details. Send the site location, plot dimensions and what you want to build.
  2. Discuss your requirements. Rooms, floors, parking, budget and anything specific your family or business needs.
  3. Plan and layout. We prepare the floor plans and site layout for you to review.
  4. Approval and working drawings. Once the plan is agreed, we prepare the drawings for approval and for the site.

Who it is for

Anyone with a plot in or around Mysuru who needs a house plan or building plan, whether you build with us or with your own contractor.

Questions about this service

Do you prepare house plans in Mysuru?

Yes. Drawings prepared for approval are part of our architectural and building planning service.
