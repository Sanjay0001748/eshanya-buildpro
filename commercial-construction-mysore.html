Commercial building construction

Commercial Construction in Mysore (Mysuru)

Shops, offices and commercial buildings in and around Mysore, designed, estimated and built under the supervision of our own structural engineers.

Get a Free Quote +91 97424 81544

A commercial building has to earn its cost back, so the questions are sharper than for a house: how much usable floor area the plot can give, what the structure will cost, and how soon the building can open. As architects and civil and structural engineers, we answer those questions on paper before any money is spent on site.

We prepare the plan and the structural design, work out the steel and concrete quantities, give you an itemised estimate, and then take up the construction and supervise it. One office is responsible for the drawings, the budget and the work.

What our commercial construction service covers

  • Planning for shops, offices and commercial buildings
  • Structural design for larger spans and multiple floors
  • Steel and concrete quantities worked out in advance
  • Itemised cost estimate before construction begins
  • Construction of the building
  • Site visits and checks at every key stage

How it works

  1. Enquiry and site visit. Tell us the site location, plot size and the kind of building you need.
  2. Plan and structure. We prepare the plan and the structural design for the building.
  3. Estimate. You receive the quantities and an itemised cost estimate.
  4. Construction and handover. We build and supervise the work through to handover.

Who it is for

Owners planning a shop, an office, a clinic, a showroom or a mixed commercial and residential building in or around Mysore.
