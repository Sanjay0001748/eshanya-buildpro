Process

From footing to facade

Scroll to watch one building go up in the order it is designed and built.

Stage 1 of 4 · Foundation
  1. 01

    Foundation

    The site and the loads above decide the footing. Foundations are sized before anything is drawn in elevation.

  2. 02

    Structural frame

    Columns and beams are designed as one frame, so every load has a clear path to the ground.

  3. 03

    Floor slabs

    Slabs are worked out floor by floor, and the steel and concrete quantities feed straight into the estimate.

  4. 04

    Finished building

    Elevation, interiors and supervision on site carry the drawings through to the completed structure.

The practice

About the firm

Eshanya BuildPro is a Mysuru practice of architects, civil and structural engineering consultants. The same office that draws the plan also designs the structure, prepares the estimate and supervises the work on site.

That keeps the drawings, the steel schedule and the budget in step with each other from the first layout to handover.

Eshanya is the north-east, the direction traditional Indian building practice treats as the most auspicious. The logo marks it with N and E under the roof.
