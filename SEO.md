# SEO plan for eshanyabuildpro.com

Each page targets one group of related searches. Both spellings, Mysuru and Mysore, are used because people search for both.
The keywords are chosen from how people commonly phrase these searches; they are not measured search volumes. Check real numbers in Google Search Console once the site has been live for a few weeks.

## What is built into the site

- A unique title, description and main heading on every page, each with its keyword and the city
- One page per service, linked from the homepage service cards, the footer and each other
- Structured data: business details, services, breadcrumbs and FAQs
- An "Areas we serve" section naming Mysuru localities
- Descriptive alt text on every photo, a sitemap and a robots file
- Fast, mobile-friendly pages

## What only you can do (this matters as much as the site)

1. **Google Business Profile.** Create or claim it at google.com/business with exactly the same name, address and phone number as the site. Add photos, services and opening hours. This is what puts you on Google Maps for "near me" searches.
2. **Google Search Console.** Add eshanyabuildpro.com at search.google.com/search-console and submit `https://eshanyabuildpro.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. **Reviews.** Ask every finished client for a Google review. Then add a few to the testimonials section (see README).
4. **Local listings.** List the business on Justdial, Sulekha and IndiaMART with the same name, address and phone number.
5. **New content.** Add each completed project with photos, locality and size, and write the guide articles (cost of building a house in Mysuru, cost per square foot, construction process).
6. **Social pages.** Create the pages, post project photos regularly and add the links to the site footer.
